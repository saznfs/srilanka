Misty Mountains

Explore Sri Lanka’s misty mountains during your holiday. The cold ‘Hill Country’ comprises of picturesque towns from Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella and more. Enjoy strolls through tea estates and visit a tea factory complete with a delightful tea tasting experience or hike to the peak of the island’s highest mountains and tallest waterfalls. As you explore these mountainous towns, you’ll learn of their rich history and legendary tales, witness a plethora of flora and fauna from leopards to sambar deer.


Popular Mountainous Towns in Sri Lanka

  • Nuwara Eliya
  • Kandy
  • Ella
  • Haputale
  • Matale
  • Hatton
  • Pattipola
  • Meemure
